# P003 walkthrough preview — proposed, not playable yet

The current downloadable build is P002. Follow [WALKTHROUGH.md](../WALKTHROUGH.md)
for that version. The steps below describe the proposed P003 chapter; no P003
patch or screenshots exist yet.

1. Back up your P002 game save before upgrading. Keep that backup if you might
   want to undo the city's story changes later.
2. Complete the warehouse boulder puzzle and defeat Mister R. A loss or an
   unfinished battle does not unlock the Office passage. See the current guide
   for the exact puzzle, moves, team and retry instructions.
3. Interact with the damaged warehouse sign and accept the service-passage offer.
4. In the Office service room, speak to the attendant. Choose **Yes** to shut
   down local Nexus communications. Choose **No** to postpone it; you can return.
5. After the shutdown message, leave through the normal south door. It returns
   you to the warehouse. Use the return route in the current walkthrough to
   reach Mandarin City.
6. Talk to the citizen who previously said the islands were overrun. Their
   original aftermath dialogue now says they should get back to work. Enter
   the Pokémon Center: the trapped scientist and hostile NPC have departed.
7. Save normally. Returning to the attendant gives the completed message.
   There is no extra battle, item or badge in this chapter. Main Office floors,
   Rudy's fight, Route60 and the next island remain future work.

Downgrading the ROM does not undo the shutdown in your save. Restore your
pre-P003 backup if you want the old city state. Before removing the Office room
feature entirely, exit to the original warehouse and save there.

The shutdown, attendant wording and hostile NPC departure are new design.
The citizen dialogue and scientist's flag-controlled departure survive in the
original ROM. The final guide will replace this preview only after these steps
have been tested in the implemented game, with observed screenshots.

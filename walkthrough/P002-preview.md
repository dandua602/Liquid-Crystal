# P002 walkthrough preview — not playable in P001

**Proposed content awaiting review. No P002 ROM has been built.** These are the
planned instructions for the next small slice, not directions to an area that
already exists in the P001 patch.

## Planned route

1. Finish P001 by defeating Mister R in the Route 59 warehouse.
2. Return to the damaged sign, face it and press **A**.
3. Read the original clue, then choose **YES** when offered entry into the
   **Office Block service room**. Choosing NO keeps you in the warehouse.
4. Explore the small service room. The attendant explains that the main office
   is closed and that the passage leads back out.
5. Leave by the room's normal southern door to return to the warehouse.
6. Save normally. You may enter the service room again from the sign.

This proposed slice has no extra puzzle, battle, item, badge or rank. The city
front door and later Office floors remain unchanged. Once implemented and
tested, these instructions will move into the playable walkthrough with actual
screenshots and any corrected directions.

Before removing a future P002 patch, return to the original warehouse and save.
A save left in the proposed new room would not load in P001-only/original ROMs.
